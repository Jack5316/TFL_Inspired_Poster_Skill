# Nine historical phases

Use this phase model for precise historical routing. Treat dates as design anchors, not rigid boundaries. Combine at most two phases and state which controls structure versus surface.

## P1 — Pictorial birth, 1908–1914

- Story: friendly access, destination discovery, reassuring first-time passengers.
- Structure: one narrative vignette, period figures, illustrated frame, generous caption block.
- Technique: hand-drawn lithography, crayon or ink texture, warm paper grain.
- Palette: ochre `#C89B3C`, cream `#F2E7C9`, brick red `#9E3B2F`, deep green `#2F5D3A`, muted navy `#2E3A4E`.
- Type: ornamental display lettering with restrained supporting copy.
- Tone: avuncular, humorous, reassuring.

## P2 — Avant-garde awakening, 1915–1925

- Story: metropolitan speed, crowds, escalators, electricity, modern life.
- Structure: dynamic diagonals, fragmented figures, overlapping planes, compressed depth.
- Technique: angular gouache, airbrush, lithographic grain, Cubist or Vorticist geometry.
- Palette: dusty blue `#5C748C`, mustard `#C99A2E`, rust `#A63A28`, black `#1C1C1E`, cream `#EDE4CE`.
- Type: condensed sans integrated into the geometry.
- Tone: energetic, experimental, forward-looking.

## P3 — Art Deco golden age, 1925–1939

- Story: leisure, nightlife, shopping, sport, gardens, seaside, machine-age travel.
- Structure: sunbursts, heroic low angles, streamlined curves, stepped skyline, monumental form.
- Technique: opaque lithography, controlled airbrush gradients, occasional photomontage logic.
- Palette: vivid orange `#F26A21`, sunshine yellow `#F7C948`, deep navy `#12284B`, cream `#F4E8C8`, scarlet `#D62828`.
- Type: geometric display caps, diagonal setting, curve setting, or hard drop shadow.
- Tone: glamorous, aspirational, optimistic.

## P4 — Wartime wit and utility, 1939–1945

- Story: etiquette, queues, blackout safety, morale, public-service instruction.
- Structure: small comic scene with one human mistake or action carrying the message.
- Technique: cartoon ink outline, flat watercolour fill, economical print.
- Palette: slate `#5A6A72`, dull red `#8A3A32`, olive `#6B6A3C`, cream `#E6DEC3`, near-black `#1A1A1A`.
- Type: hand-lettered rhyme or informal brush capitals.
- Tone: wry, admonishing, humane.

## P5 — Festival modernism, 1945–1959

- Story: family leisure, rebuilding, suburbia, countryside, democratic optimism.
- Structure: one emblematic idea, smiling geometric figures, symbolic sun or simplified nature.
- Technique: flat lithography, paper-cut collage, symbolic reduction.
- Palette: teal `#2E8C8C`, coral `#F28A6B`, butter yellow `#F5D96C`, sky blue `#7FB4D8`, soft grey `#D6D3CC`.
- Type: cheerful mid-century sans with a short footline.
- Tone: optimistic, kindly, family-oriented.

## P6 — Mosaic and pop transition, 1960–1979

- Story: civic identity, bus travel, sightseeing, crafted modernity.
- Structure: iconic centred vehicle or object, large landmark blocks, confident single-word headline.
- Technique: tessera-like mosaic translated into print, screenprint grain, early photographic collage.
- Palette: tomato red `#E23D28`, teal `#1F8A8A`, ochre `#C89B3C`, black `#1B1B1B`, cream `#EDE4CE`.
- Type: clean 1960s grotesk, broad and direct.
- Tone: civic, crafted, quietly pop.

## P7 — Corporate identity and photography, 1980–1999

- Story: metropolitan confidence, network identity, speed, night-time city movement.
- Structure: photographic hero image plus one strong colour band or modular type block.
- Technique: high-contrast colour photography, offset print, controlled motion blur.
- Palette: vivid red `#D33A2C`, civic blue `#173C8F`, deep night navy `#0B1F3A`, cool grey `#BEC5CF`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Type: large neutral grotesk with strict horizontal alignment.
- Tone: branded, informational, metropolitan.
- Boundary: replace official marks and real station names with fictional identity unless authorized assets are supplied.

## P8 — Fine-art commissioning and neo-deco revival, 2000–2019

- Story: local pride, culture, public art, seeing London afresh.
- Structure: bold landmark reduction, generous negative space, one crafted image system.
- Technique: linocut or relief print, enamel-like flat geometry, artist-led map-cover logic.
- Palette: deep plum `#4A2545`, teal `#1E7A7A`, warm orange `#E07B39`, cream `#EDE4CE`, optional charcoal `#222222`.
- Type: sturdy capitals treated as printed shape.
- Tone: proud, local, celebratory.

## P9 — Digital, immersive, and generative, 2020 onward

- Story: connected journeys, programmable space, future mobility, data or environmental response.
- Structure: LED ribbon, digital gateway, platform-edge sequence, anamorphic depth, generative network.
- Technique: full-motion keyframe, pixel mesh, controlled glow, 3D illusion, rule-based variation.
- Palette: electric blue `#2B6BFF`, magenta `#E93DB8`, ultraviolet `#7A3BFF`, neon coral `#FF5C7A`, indigo-black `#0A0A1F`.
- Type: minimal sans with a stable two-second reading state.
- Tone: immersive, alive, connected.
- Boundary: use fictional data and identity; never imply real service status or reproduce the official roundel as a light effect.

## Remix rule

Use one phase for structure and another only for surface. Examples: P3 streamlined composition with P9 LED material; P5 cut-paper structure with P8 linocut texture; P7 photographic grid with P2 diagonal energy. Keep the subject and wording original.

Treat every hex value as an artistic starting point, not an official specification. Adjust contrast for accessibility and avoid rebuilding a current operator's complete colour system.
